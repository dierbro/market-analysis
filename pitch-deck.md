## SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM

### 301,983 Property Managers Are Stuck in the Stone Age

**Pain Point 1: Manual, Error-Prone Processes**
- 87% of German property managers use Excel and paper
- Invoice generation takes hours, not minutes
- Owner communications scattered across email, phone, paper
- Financial reporting requires manual data entry

**Pain Point 2: Legacy Software Is Broken**
- €500-2,000/month pricing locks out micro-enterprises
- Enterprise-focused (requires 500+ units)
- Poor user experience (built in 2000s)
- No mobile support (property managers work on-site)

**Pain Point 3: Scaling Is Impossible**
- Can't grow beyond 200-300 units efficiently
- Hiring more staff = linear costs
- Succession crisis: Can't sell business without systems

**The Market:**
- 30,486 companies in Germany alone
- 26,523 are micro-enterprises (1-6 employees)
- Managing 12 million properties worth €15.4B annually

---

## SLIDE 3: THE SOLUTION

### Modern Property Management Platform Built for Europe

**What We Do:**
Cloud-based SaaS platform that automates the entire property management workflow for micro-enterprises across 6 European countries.

**Key Features:**
1. **Automated Billing** → Generate invoices in seconds, not hours
2. **Owner Portal** → Owners access documents, invoices, payments 24/7
3. **Compliance** → Country-specific legal requirements built-in (WEG, copropriété)
4. **Mobile-First** → Manage properties from anywhere (iOS/Android apps)
5. **Integrations** → DATEV (Germany), banks, accounting systems

**Why It Works:**
✅ **10x cheaper** than legacy software (€49-299/month vs €500-2,000)
✅ **Built for small firms** (not enterprises)
✅ **Country-specific** (German for Germany, French for France, not translated)
✅ **2-4x efficiency gains** through automation
